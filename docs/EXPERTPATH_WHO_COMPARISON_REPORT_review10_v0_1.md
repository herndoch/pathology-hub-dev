## 1. Pleomorphic Adenoma

**Our page:** ⚠️ Solid section skeleton and correct organ, but thinner than ExpertPath and missing PA-defining molecular depth; Critic `revise` (missing benign-metastasizing facts; DDx sclerosing polycystic adenoma).

Missing essential criteria:
- PLAG1 / HMGA2 rearrangements (~70% EP; WHO recurrent fusions) not in Essential criteria (ours is morphology-only)
- Capsule differences (parotid thick capsule vs minor glands often unencapsulated) under-emphasized

- Carcinoma ex-pleomorphic adenoma
- Adenoid cystic carcinoma (vs cellular PA)
- Polymorphous adenocarcinoma
- Epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma / myoepithelial carcinoma

- Intercalated duct adenoma
- (Critic also flagged sclerosing polycystic adenoma — present in our DDx text after revision, but thin)

Factual errors / entity conflation: none major in prose; gallery mostly on-entity (primary/recurrent PA cut surfaces).

Off-organ content found: none

Figure quality: Mostly relevant PA gross/histo thumbnails; far fewer labeled architectural variants than ExpertPath’s gallery; not clearly tied to named features (plasmacytoid myoepithelium, chondromyxoid stroma, pseudopods).

Depth verdict: adequate

## Summary

**Comparable to ExpertPath:** **0 / 10**.  
**Adequate (useful skeleton, not ExpertPath-depth):** **4** — Pleomorphic Adenoma, DCIS, Clear Cell RCC, Lipoma.  
**Shallow (needs major rebuild):** **6** — Adenoid Cystic Carcinoma, Secretory Carcinoma, Prostatic Acinar Adenocarcinoma, DLBCL NOS, Melanoma overview/NOS, Colonic adenocarcinoma arising in adenoma.

**Most work needed (top 3):**
1. **Colonic Adenocarcinoma arising in adenoma** — wrong DDx gravity (missing pseudoinvasion), missing Haggitt/Kikuchi, adenosquamous figure/entity conflation; WHO has no dedicated page so ExpertPath must drive the template.
2. **Prostatic Acinar Adenocarcinoma** — absence of Gleason/Grade Groups + ASAP/benign-mimic DDx; Skene-gland off-organ figures.
3. **Adenoid Cystic Carcinoma** (tie with Secretory Carcinoma) — cystadenoma gallery contamination and missing biphasic/basement-membrane/HGT depth; Secretory similarly harmed by thyroid-SC figure + molecular listed as merely desirable.

**Cross-cutting patterns:**
- **Figure pipeline pollution:** galleries repeatedly include DDx/related/wrong-entity images (cystadenoma→ACC; thyroid SC→salivary SC; Skene→prostate; CDC/CCSK/PEComa→CCRCC; adenosquamous→malignant polyp; myolipoma→lipoma). This is the strongest systemic defect.
- **Essential criteria too morphologic/generic:** definitional molecular (PLAG1/HMGA2, ETV6, MYB) and reporting systems (Gleason, Haggitt/Kikuchi, Hans/COO) are missing or demoted to Desirable.
- **DDx lists undershoot ExpertPath:** common practical differentials (ASAP, pseudoinvasion, UDH/ADH, HPV multiphenotypic sinonasal Ca, microsecretory adenocarcinoma) often absent; odd rare entities sometimes present instead.
- **Uniform Critic verdict:** all 10 pages `verdict: revise` with revision_applied true — critic catches some issues but does not restore ExpertPath depth.
- **Length/depth gap:** our synthesized bodies ~2.3–3.2k characters vs ExpertPath pages with dozens–100+ images and multi-subsection Key Facts; section headers match the template, content density does not.
- **Melanoma NOS / Colon arising-in-adenoma** are structurally hard: ExpertPath/WHO organize by subtype or split adenoma vs adenocarcinoma — our leaf labels need explicit overview or malignant-polyp templates, not generic carcinoma fill.
